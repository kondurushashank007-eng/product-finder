Community Commerce Initiative

Connecting You Directly with Local Stores

Local Product Finder was built to solve a simple yet frustrating modern problem: you need a product today, but you have no easy way of knowing whether a neighborhood shop has it on their shelf or how much they charge without making dozens of phone calls or wasting trips.

Our Core Principles

1. Trustworthy Inventory Data

We never fabricate listings, invent stock numbers, or estimate distances. Every listed product, price, and quantity is managed directly by authorized store owners or imported through official catalog updates.

2. Clear Freshness Indicators

Inventory records include the exact time they were last updated. If an item has not been updated in over 14 days, a clear warning badge alerts the customer to call the store before visiting.

3. Supporting Main Street Retail

Local independent businesses are the backbone of vibrant communities. By making in-store inventories discoverable online, we level the playing field against distant e-commerce giants.

4. AI Without Hallucination

Our AI Search interprets natural language queries (extracting product terms, unit sizes, price caps, and proximity preferences) to filter genuine database records, asking clarifying questions when ambiguous.
